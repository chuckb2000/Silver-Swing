Where to look, in order (the course chat at intake):
1. The club's own website: a scorecard PDF or image, or a course page with
   yardages per hole (search "Astoria Golf & Country Club scorecard").
2. BlueGolf (course.bluegolf.com, the club's "detailed scorecard"; closed to
   the sandbox: the developer can save it as PDF).
3. GolfPass "scorecard and layout", stymie.golf, the Oregon Golf
   Association's Explore Oregon Golf page, GHIN / USGA Course Rating
   database for ratings and slopes (men's and women's).
4. Oregon Coast Invitational coverage (tournament yardages per hole, if
   published).
5. Failing all: a photo of the printed card (developer).
