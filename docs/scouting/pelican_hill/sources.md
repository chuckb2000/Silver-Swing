## Notes from memory (unverified; check in the follow-up pass)

- Address: The Resort at Pelican Hill, 22701 Pelican Hill Road South, Newport
  Coast, CA 92657; owner The Irvine Company. Box centre 33.592, -117.848 is
  placed by memory: inland of Pacific Coast Highway, between Newport Coast
  Drive (Corona del Mar side) and Crystal Cove State Park (Los Trancos side).
- Ocean North opened in 1993. Both courses were closed and reworked by Fazio
  around 2007, during the building of the resort, which opened in late 2008. The
  assignment gives "opened 1991, redesigned by Fazio 2007".
- Landscape (also the assignment's notes): canyons of coastal sage scrub, ice
  plant banks, ocean bluffs, palms, and the Italian stone pines that the resort's
  architecture is known for.
