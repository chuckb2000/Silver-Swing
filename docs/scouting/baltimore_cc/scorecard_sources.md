## What is needed

holes; ratings and slopes, men's and women's; the printed totals to add up
against. The same for the West Course if it is ever built as a second layout.

## Where to look (in order)

1. The club's printed East Course card: a photo from the developer (the club
   is private; its website may keep the card behind a members' login).
2. The club website's public pages (course or golf page, sometimes a
   scorecard image or PDF).
3. BlueGolf (`course.bluegolf.com`, Baltimore Country Club East, "detailed
   scorecard"), GolfPass, Stymie (`stymie.golf/golf-courses/maryland/...`),
   OpenGolfAPI (`courses.opengolfapi.org/courses/MD/...`, from OSM).
4. USGA Course Rating database (ncrdb.usga.org) and the Maryland State Golf
   Association (ratings and slopes per tee, men's and women's).
5. Tournament records with hole yardages: the 1988 U.S. Women's Open (USGA
   records), the Senior Players Championships played at Five Farms
   (PGA Tour Champions; believed 2007-2010, unverified) and any USGA or
   amateur championship held there since.
