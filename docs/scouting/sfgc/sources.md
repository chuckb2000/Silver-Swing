## What was searched but not found

- **Gil Hanse at SFGC:** no source, including Golf Digest's and Links' restoration
  histories, mentions it. Every source names Doak and Urbina (2001 greens; 2006 holes
  13-15 and trees) as the latest architects.
- **The club's own scorecard:** none online.
- **Video games:** SFGC is not in *PGA Championship Golf 1999* or *Tiger Woods PGA
  Tour 06* (their lists were checked for Pasatiempo). A general search found nothing.
- **OSM:** no openstreetmap.org page for the course came up in search, so coverage is
  unknown until the Overpass export.
- **Lidar:** the web-search budget ran out (shared by every agent this turn) before
  the 3DEP project and the NOAA DAV dataset for San Francisco could be looked up.
  DATA_REQUEST.md items 2b and 3 pick them up from the services themselves.
- **A routing map:** none found. GolfClubAtlas mentions a Tillinghast routing sketch
  that hangs in the clubhouse (reproduced in *The Course Beautiful*), but it isn't
  online.
- **Water:** no source mentions a pond or creek on the course. Lake Merced lies to
  the north-west, outside it.
