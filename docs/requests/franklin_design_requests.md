## 1. A logo for `franklin`

**What:** an original logo for course id `franklin` in `assets-src/logos.py`,
like the other planned courses (the developer asked the design chat to draw

**Course facts that may help:** Franklin Country Club, Franklin, Norfolk
County, Massachusetts; founded 1899 as the Franklin Rod and Gun Club, 18
holes laid out in the early 1920s (Wayne Stiles and John Van Kleek, per
countryclubmag.com); a wooded New England parkland course on the
Franklin-Wrentham line, with Uncas Brook running past the clubhouse and the
par-3 14th played across Uncas Pond. No club crest or branding may be used
(CLAUDE.md 14).
