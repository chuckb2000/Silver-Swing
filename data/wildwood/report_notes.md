## Other open items (Wildwood Golf Course)

Built 2026-10-24. Standing defaults (COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1): no Blue on the card, so the Black tees (5,853 yd, the set closest to 6,300); all water red; out of bounds = the OSM course boundary (way 428675780), kept as mapped (developer, 2026-10-24); trees from DOGAMI lidar; `pnw` theme; wind 1-7 mph. Each item has a recommendation.

1. **Holes 6, 9, 14 and 18 measure short of the Black card** (6: 125 vs 131, 9: 295 vs 311, 14: 156 vs 183, 18: 329 vs 360). The developer marked each Black tee in the tracing desk (data/wildwood/desk/) and accepted the differences ("don't worry about the discrepancies on those holes", 2026-10-24). The "FAIL, needs a decision" lines above are s06's standard wording; they are decided. Recommendation: none.
2. **Driving range not built.** OSM doesn't map the range between the 10th and 18th (shown on the club's 2025 course map). The desk offered a first-pass outline from the 2024 photo ("R" page); it has not been saved, so the range plays as rough and in bounds. Recommendation: save the outline in the desk (it becomes golf=driving_range, out of bounds), or accept it as rough.
3. **Downhill par 3s and the auto club** (holes 3, 6, 12; tees 14-17.5 m above their greens): the game picks the club by horizontal distance, so a perfect shot with it flies the green. A game change, requested from the design chat (data/wildwood/design_request.md). `game/tests/unit/wildwood.test.ts` lists hole 3 as a known exception.
4. **Water:** red penalty areas everywhere (4.1 default): the three OSM ponds (`golf=water_hazard`, tagged `penalty=red` in edits.json), the creek strip OSM maps as `natural=water` between the 2nd green and the 5th tee (`red_water_osm`, bed kept with `flatten=no`), the creek channels from the lidar along OSM's stream lines (developer approved, 2026-10-24; inside the boundary only, 3 m clear of greens and tees), and the swimming pool behind the house north of the 16th tee (developer: "that would be funny"). OSM's stream across the 14th was left out: no channel in the lidar and none on the club map.
5. **Out of bounds close to play:** the OSM boundary follows the cleared turf, so a ball just into the woods is out of bounds on most holes (fairways within 4-9 m of the line on 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 14, 15 and 18; tees within 5 m on 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15 and 16). The developer kept the line as mapped. The creek left of 4 and 7 lies outside it and stays out of bounds.
6. **The house and pool inside the boundary** (north of the 16th tee and 17th green): the buildings are obstructions with free relief; the pool is a red penalty area (item 4).
7. **Trees:** the DOGAMI canopy model is older than the 2024 photo; `trees_need_ortho` dropped 923 lidar crowns the photo no longer shows (among them a row down the 9th fairway). `remove_wires` took the utility line along US 30 out of the canopy model (4 pieces, 186 m). Two single trees were removed for the standing default (perfect tee-shot line): a 24 m crown 16 m in front of the 2nd tee and a 46 m fir over the 7th's line 58 m out (`drop_trees`). Tree types come from crown shape only (no leaf-off data).
8. **Tees placed by the course chat:** on 3 the OSM line starts on a long box (w1283372592, 141-174 yd) that s06 skipped by centroid; it is tagged hole 3 / Black (edits.json). The 6th and 9th boxes, tagged the same way at first, were replaced by the developer's desk marks.
9. **Scorecard:** the club's own card (website image, read 2026-10-24); every row adds up and the handicaps run 1-18. GolfPass lists 68.9/124 for one unnamed set; the card's Black men's 69.1/127 is used. Recommendation: none needed.
10. **Daily rotation:** `game/tests/unit/player.test.ts` requires every course on the list to be in `DAILY_ROSTER`, so it fails on this branch until the design chat adds `wildwood` (COURSE_BUILD.md 0).
