## Other open items (TPC Boston)

Built by a course chat on 2026-10-20/21 with the standing defaults (docs/COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1). The developer's calls (2026-10-20): Black and Gold tees with Black the default, the club's 2024 card (par 72, hole 14 a par 4), the 1,792 x 2,304 m world, theme `bayside`, a baby blue lederhosen logo. The developer went to bed during the build and asked for it to be finished overnight; the items below are the course chat's calls, each with a recommendation.

1. **Scorecard:** the club's 2024 printed card (release `data-tpc-boston`, `2024_Scorecard.pdf`), with its ratings: Black 72.1/139, Gold 74.6/144. Every row adds up to its printed total. OSM, Wikipedia and Stymie carry the tour card (par 73, hole 14 a par 5, another stroke index); OSM's par on 14 is set to 4 in `edits.json`. s06's open-item lines call the card's stroke index "GolfPass" (fixed wording in the shared pipeline); it is the club card's. Recommendation: keep the card.
2. **Three tee boxes assigned by tag** (`edits.json`, the course chat's call; s06's lines say "the developer" for any tagged box): hole 6 Black (w677741698, 428 yd from its centre against the card's 429, 29 m beside the line where s06 searches 25 m); hole 3 Gold (w1239401660, a 42 x 21 m tee complex spanning 147-187 yd that is also the Black box); hole 16 Black (w1110722253, a 60 x 23 m complex spanning 90-144 yd). OSM has no separate box for those two sets; s06 moves the tee to the back of each complex: 3 Gold 180 yd (card 184), 16 Black 140 yd (card 144). Recommendation: accept.
3. **Every hole within 3 % of the card after the tee moves** (`adjustments.json`: Black 1, 4, 8, 16 and Gold 2, 3, 11, each along the middle of its real box). Black 6,436 yd vs 6,454 (-0.3 %); Gold 6,972 vs 6,990 (-0.3 %).
4. **Lidar tee pads not used.** `propose_tees.py` found pads near the card distance for Gold 3 and Black 16, but 117-166 m off the hole lines (`proposed/tees_lidar.png`); the long OSM tee complexes cover both sets.
5. **Every penalty area is red** (standing default): the 17 OSM `golf=water_hazard` ponds and marshes (retagged `penalty=red`), plus, through `red_water_osm`, a 1.0 ha pond mapped only as `natural=water` (w1239408943, east of 8, 98 m from the hole) and four wetlands mapped only as `natural=wetland`: the marsh under hole 11's line (w1239419367, 0.14 ha, a forced carry on the par 3) and three small swamps and a marsh 11-35 m from holes 4 and 5 (w1239391361/62/66). Without that they would play as rough. Recommendation: keep; if the club plays any of the wetlands as ground, one config line takes each out.
6. **47 lidar trees inside OSM penalty areas were dropped** (spec 5.4), mostly in the marsh near hole 5 (w421116009, 20 trees of 6-18 m) and along the pond by 18 (w678172528, 13 trees of 18-20 m, where the OSM outline takes in the treed bank). Those spots draw as open water. Five more on fairways and tees. Recommendation: accept.
7. **Norton Country Club** (74 m west, inside the world): its boundary, hole lines and the greens beyond the world edge are dropped, and its nine `golf=water_hazard` ponds are redrawn as plain water, so the whole course plays as out of bounds (the game checks water before out of bounds; as hazards they would have been yellow penalty areas outside the TPC boundary).
8. **Pins** at the centre of each green (`make_edits.py`); OSM's six pins are not on hole greens. The game adds up to three more per green (`pins.test.ts` passes).
9. **Greens:** fitted tilts are clamped to 2.5 % on eight greens; the whole-green maximum slope reaches 5.5 % (hole 2) and 5.2 % (hole 6) where the green blends into its surround; every pin area is <= 1.5 %.
10. **Practice greens:** three by the clubhouse (w1110732539, w1239419341, w1239419343) are wrong greens (relief required). The driving range is out of bounds and drawn as a striped range.
11. **Paths and clubhouse:** the 14 `golf=path` lines are cart paths (copies with new ids), and the clubhouse (`golf=clubhouse` + `building=yes`) is drawn and plays as a building.
12. **Trees** from the 2021 USGS Central Eastern Massachusetts lidar (NOAA job J1473646, flown early April 2021, leaf-off): 49,992 trees, 8,437 inside the course; 45 % read as conifers from the leaf-off returns. Median 21 m, tallest 35 m. Every tee shot at the middle of the fairway and straight down the line clears them (s06b dropped none). The export is 18.6 MB (Franklin 17.3 MB).
13. **Elevation:** the USGS 3DEP elevation service's 1 m export of the world + 100 m (31.5-54.4 m). The first upload (two full tiles) arrived as 8-bit JPEG pictures and was replaced; the pictures are still in the release and can be deleted.
14. **Tee sets not built:** Championship (7,282), White (6,035) and Blue (5,147) are in `scorecard.csv`; Blended (6,213) is printed as a total only.
15. **Course card:** see `assets-src/cards/tpc_boston.json` (course chat's pick).
16. **Logo:** a baby blue pair of lederhosen (developer's subject; `assets-src/course_logos/tpc_boston.py`). Original drawing.
