## Other open items (Rock Creek Park Golf)

The developer's calls on 2026-10-22: build the 14 holes on the card ("let's roll with that"), the `piedmont` theme in mid-autumn, the Joaquin Miller Cabin logo, and the tee boxes of holes 2, 5 and 14 (marked on the photo). Everything else below is the course chat's call under the standing defaults (COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1), each with a recommendation.

1. **Routing.** OSM maps 9 hole lines with no number or par. The 14 holes come from the 1927 Flynn plan and the NCPC 2023 plans (fitted to the OSM greens), the DC 2023 orthophoto and the GolfDC course map the developer supplied, which shows the current routing; they match it hole for hole (1-11, then old 15, 17 and 18 as 12-14). Lines for 2, 4, 5, 9 and 11 are new (`make_holes.py`); 6 and 12 keep the OSM lines with the bend moved to the fairway in the landing zone. Recommendation: keep.
2. **Three holes flagged (3-8 %), none failing.** Hole 1: 282 yd against 302 (-6.8 %): the mapped 1st tee ends at the car park, so the tee can't go further back. Hole 3: 155 against 160 (-3.3 %), the tee at the back of its box. Hole 4: 165 against 173 (-4.8 %), from a small lidar pad by the Joyce Road bend. Recommendation: accept all three.
3. **Tee boxes.** Holes 1 and 3 use OSM boxes tagged for them by the course chat (the s06 line above says "developer"; it was the course chat). Holes 4, 6, 12 and 9/11 use raised pads in the lidar DEM (`propose_tees.py`); 9 and 11 share the pad beside the 10th green, which s06 gives to 11, so 9 plays from the line start on the same pad. Holes 2, 5 and 14 use the boxes the developer marked on the photo. Holes 7, 8, 10 and 13 use OSM boxes.
4. **The closed holes are rough.** The greens of old 12, 13, 14 and 16 and the fairways of old 14 and 16 (closed since 2015) are dropped, so nothing out there plays as a wrong green. Recommendation: keep.
5. **The practice green beside the clubhouse is a wrong green** (relief required), as on every course.
6. **Water: three stream channels are red penalty areas** (all water red), from the lidar channels along OSM's stream lines (`propose_channels.py`): across the front of the 13th green (old 17, by the wetland), left of the 14th (old 18; the "Stream" on the 1927 plan) and one in the closed north-west. Rock Creek itself is outside the boundary (out of bounds). No ponds.
7. **Trees: 8,546 from the DC lidar surface model (2,004 inside the boundary).** Rock Creek's woods are hardwood; the crown-shape split calls 76 % of the crowns "conifer" (tall, narrow tulip poplars), and the `piedmont` theme draws that class as tulip poplars (a drawing choice; physics treats both classes alike). Removed: 6 crowns standing on fairways or a tee pad, and 3 in front of the 6th tee (19-21 m, 27-42 m out) that a perfect drive at the fairway would hit. The lidar predates the 2024- renovation, which removes 1,139 trees; the course built is the pre-renovation layout, so they stay.
8. **Hole 8's fairway** between the tee and the OSM fairway is traced from the mown stripes on the 2023 photo (`propose_trace.py`); the GolfDC map shows 8's fairway from tee to green. Recommendation: keep.
9. **Greens on a hilly site.** Fitted tilts run up to 7 % (12th); every green is clamped to 2.5 %, and the steepest spot on a green is 3.2-5.7 % (5.7 on the 3rd, 5.5 on the 1st, 5.4 on the 5th), above the ~3.5 % guide, while every pin area stays within 1.5 %. Recommendation: accept unless putting plays badly.
10. **No course rating or slope published**, so the White tees are "not rated" (no handicap differentials). The card prints the Red total as 3623 (the holes add to 3523) and stroke index 8 twice (holes 2 and 14).
11. **The DC lidar's year is unknown** (as for East Potomac and Langston); only the attribution text would change.
