## Other open items (Sheep Ranch)

Built 2026-10-21 together with Pacific Dunes and Old Macdonald (shared downloads, GitHub release pacific-dunes-data). Standing defaults (COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1): all water red (there is none), out of bounds = the course polygon w823626858, trees from lidar; wind 8-16 mph and the bandon_dunes theme with gorse as the developer set for Bandon Dunes. **Tees: Black** (developer, 2026-10-21): the OSM hole lines start on the tees, and measured from there the course matches the Black card (6,677 yd from the line starts against 6,636), not Green (6,245, the 4.1 default). The items below are the course chat's calls, each with a recommendation.

1. **Tee boxes.** OSM maps only three tiny tee markers (13-58 m2). Where no mapped box at the line start measures within 8 % of the Black card, a 12 x 8 m box aligned with the line stands on the line start (`make_tees_linestart.py`; 16 holes; the markers serve holes 1 and 16). The box size is the course chat's. Recommendation: keep.
2. **Hole 3: the line starts beside the 2nd green, 183 yd out against a 120-yd card (+52 %).** The box stands where the Black distance falls on the line, 57 m ahead of the line start, on mown ground (118 yd). Recommendation: confirm, or say where the tee is.
3. **Flags, measured from the line starts** (after s06 moved each tee inside its box): hole 2 334/318 yd (+5.2 %), 12 417/440 (-5.2 %). Recommendation: accept.
4. **Double green 3/16 on the point.** OSM maps one green (w865788982, 3,147 m2) with a pin for each hole; it is cut in two along the perpendicular bisector of the two pins (`split_double_greens.py`), so each hole has its own part, pin and putting views (as at Langdon Farms). The parts are shaped as two greens, which leaves a seam between them (a design-chat request).
5. **The lidar was flown in 2008, twelve years before Sheep Ranch opened** (2020; the headland held an earlier 13-green layout). Greens are shaped by script and tees levelled anyway. The lidar is DOGAMI's OLC South Coast project (DOGAMI labels it 2009; the USGS data reports give the flights as June-October 2008). It is the only survey covering the three courses: DOGAMI's 2020 South Coast project has no data here, and the USGS OR_Western_D23 point cloud starts at about 43.223 N (checked 2026-10-21). 558 lidar trees whose crowns are not canopy in the 2024 photo were dropped (`trees_need_ortho`).
6. **The sea:** the 2008 lidar has no data over the ocean, which the world's north-west corner reaches; `fill_sea.py` (run by `make fetch`) gives those cells the water-line height, and s04 flattens them with the sea. Scenery only.
7. **Gorse:** 13,668 bushes over 15.9 ha of scrub classified in the 2024 orthophoto (4.3 ha inside the boundary), on the gorse surface (17).
8. **No-play zones** (the card's local rule on 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 15, 16 and 17) are not modelled, as at Bandon Dunes.
9. **The driving range** north-east of the course (inside the world) is out of bounds and drawn as a range.
10. **The north photo tile** is used from the copy sent in the chat (PNG); its original JPEG is not in the release yet (release.json).
