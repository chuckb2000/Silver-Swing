## Other open items (Indian Island)

Decisions the developer made on 2026-10-19 are in DECISIONS.md ("Indian Island"). Still open, each with a recommendation:

1. **Holes 7, 13 and 15 play 3-5 % short of the Blue card (flags, none over 8 %).** Measured from the back of the developer's Blue tee boxes: 7 415/429 (-3.3 %), 13 339/353 (-3.9 %), 15 462/485 (-4.7 %). Hole 7's line is OSM's; 13 and 15 come from the tracing desk, and 15's dogleg bend is at the centre of the traced fairway. Recommendation: accept for play; if one feels short in the game, move its Blue tee box back in the tracing desk.
2. **Holes 9 and 11-18 were traced, not mapped.** The course chat traced greens, fairways, bunkers and tee pads from the NYS orthoimagery (2022-2025, 0.32 m) and the 2024 lidar DEM (`pipeline/propose_trace.py`). The developer corrected them and placed every Blue tee in the tracing desk (`data/indian_island/desk/`), and approved them. Hole 10's green and fairway were traced the same way; its line and tee are OSM's. Recommendation: none. To change a shape, edit it in the tracing desk and the course chat re-imports it (`pipeline/desk_to_proposed.py`).
3. **Pins** are at the centre of each green. One pin per green; nothing in the data gives daily pin sheets.
4. **Penalty areas are all red** (developer: no yellow on this course). The card's yellow stakes on holes 5, 6 and 7 are not modelled. The Peconic River and Flanders Bay count as a penalty area up to 100 m out from the shore, and the course boundary was grown to take that strip and the inlet pond in, so a ball in the bay is a red penalty, not out of bounds. Recommendation: keep.
5. **Route 105 (Cross River Drive) is out of bounds** inside the boundary (developer). It is painted as an out-of-bounds road (config `ob_highways`). Holes 17 and 18 play beside and across it. Riverside Drive, the internal roads and the parking area give free relief. The card's other OB holes (1, 9, 10) are covered by the course boundary. With Model Local Rule E-5 switched on, the drop for a ball that stops on the road is measured from where it stopped, not where it crossed the road (design-chat request in `docs/requests/indian_island_design_requests.md`). Stroke and distance, the default, is unaffected.
6. **Driving range** (between holes 1, 8 and 9 on the card's map) is not in OSM and was not traced; it plays as rough. Recommendation: leave it, or trace it in the tracing desk if it should be out of bounds like Oswego Lake's.
7. **Ranger station and park buildings** inside the boundary (clubhouse, maintenance yard next to hole 11, ranger station) are obstructions with free relief (developer: not out of bounds). The sewage plant west of holes 12 and 13 is outside the course boundary, so it is out of bounds by the boundary (developer: keep).
8. **Trees come from the 2024 NYS lidar,** flown leaf-off on 25 March 2024. The project has no vegetation class, so trees are taken from class 1 (unclassified), which also holds buildings. Trees on OSM buildings are dropped, but a few crowns sit on unmapped houses along Riverside Drive, all outside the course. Type comes from the leaf-off returns: 27 % pitch pine, 73 % oak and maple. 15,429 trees, 2,335 inside the course. Recommendation: accept.
9. **Card typos:** the White In total is printed 2,909 (holes add to 2,916), Gold Out 3,029 (2,971), Red Out 2,882 (2,821) and Red In 2,642 (2,663). Only Blue is built; Blue adds up.
10. **Course card** (COURSE_BUILD.md section 12): hole 5, the signature par 3 on the point with the Peconic behind the green, the course chat's pick. To use another green, change `assets-src/course_cards.json` and run `python3 assets-src/course_cards.py indian_island`.
