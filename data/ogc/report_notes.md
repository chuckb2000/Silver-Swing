## Other open items (Oregon Golf Club)

Decisions the developer made on 2026-10-19 are in DECISIONS.md ("Oregon Golf Club"); every item below is settled unless it says otherwise.

1. **Scorecard is GolfPass's (unverified); no printed card is available** (developer). Blue and White hole sums match GolfPass's printed Out/In totals; its tee table says White 6,081 where the holes add to 6,080, and Black 7,052 where they add to 7,070. Pars and the men's stroke index agree with the OSM hole tags on all 18 holes. Ratings used: Blue 72.5 / 136, White 69.7 / 133 (GolfPass). Still open: replace it if a club card turns up.
2. **Hole 7 fir removed** (developer, 2026-10-19): the 31 m fir 7 m in front of the green (1074.5, 1422.5) hid the pin in the game's 3/4 view (`drop_trees`; its canopy is cleared too). Hole 15's broadleaf, whose crown edge reaches the green without hiding the pin, stays.
3. **Driving range and hole 9: drawn by the developer in the tracing desk** (2026-10-19; saves in `data/ogc/desk/`, built by `pipeline/desk_to_proposed.py`). The range is 2.9 ha around targets P3-P9, out of bounds, drawn as rough with white OB stakes along its edge and no range balls (developer: the balls made the stakes hard to see); the short-game area (practice greens P1-P2) and the hitting area west of it play as rough. Hole 9 has a third fairway linking the two OSM ones; its green, bunker and yardage are unchanged. The first range edge (the OSM path loop, `tools/propose_range.py`) is superseded.
4. **Hole 16 White tee** (course chat's call, approved by the developer 2026-10-19): box w865842632 measures 377 yd against 383 (-1.5 %) but lies 30 m to the side of the OSM line, outside s06's 25 m search corridor, so it is assigned by tag in `edits.json` (s06's report line says "assigned by the developer").
5. **Flags (3-8 %), accepted by the developer (2026-10-19):**
   - Hole 5 White: 523 yd vs 504 (+3.8 %) after moving the tee to the front of its box; the next box forward measures 483 (-4.2 %, likely the White/Green combo tee).
   - Hole 10 Blue: 343 yd vs 361 (-4.9 %); no box behind the back one is mapped (Black and Blue are both 361 on the card).
   - Hole 10 White: 337 yd vs 324 (+4.0 %); it shares the Blue box, and the next box forward measures 303 (-6.4 %).
   Lidar shows no raised pads for these tees (the course's tees are cut into the hillside).
6. **Power lines removed from the canopy model** (`remove_wires`): the BPA transmission lines across the middle of the course and over the Willamette, and the distribution lines along the roads, were picked up as rows of thin 20-30 m "trees" and as 70-82 m "conifers" where the lines cross the river. 42 wire networks (about 9.5 km of conductor) were taken out before the tree search; 1,254 false trees went with them. The tallest tree is now 59 m (riverside firs). Short pieces of wire over a crown (under 40 m) can remain; none was found near play.
7. **Tree types are inferred from crown shape** (DOGAMI canopy height model, no leaf-off imagery): 46 % conifers inside the course, 96 % of trees over 30 m. The photos show Douglas fir lining the fairways with oaks and maples between, which matches.
8. **Water:** every hazard is red (developer), including the pond OSM tags yellow (w865546390). The creek in front of the 13th green, up the right side of 14 and between the 15th fairway and green (developer, 2026-10-20) is OSM stream lines only; its penalty areas follow the lidar channel along them (`propose_channels.py`, `proposed/channels_lidar.png`; 4.7-7.1 m wide, bed kept as surveyed with `flatten=no`). W7 and W8 lie partly outside the boundary; that part is out of bounds, as the boundary is (developer, 2026-10-19). The Willamette and its islands are off-course water, out of bounds. Two lidar trees standing in hazards (hole 8 ponds, Hershiser Reservoir) were dropped.
9. **World 1,280 x 2,304 m** (developer approved going past 2,048 m, and the load time). Checked in the game (headless Chromium, software WebGL, 2 CPUs): choosing the course to the hole-1 intro takes 5.0-6.5 s against 3.1-3.2 s for Oswego Lake (1,536 x 1,024 m) and 4.2-4.3 s for Langston (1,536 x 1,536 m); the JS heap after garbage collection is 44 MB (Langston 39, Oswego Lake 31); the export is 10.9 MB (Langston 10.6).
