# Spruce Peak: data the workflow couldn't get (2026-10-31)

Everything else came through GitHub Actions (`data/spruce_peak/fetch.json`, release
`data-spruce_peak`): OSM, the VCGI 2023 lidar DEM and DSM, the VCGI 0.3 m orthophoto and NAIP.

## 1. A photo of the printed scorecard (optional, recommended)

The club is private and posts no card. The build uses Stymie's per-hole rows (Blue 5,887 yd,
matching 18Birdies' total); GolfPass prints a different Blue row (5,824) and a third total
(6,041). A photo would settle the Blue row and the stroke index of 10 and 18.

1. Ask the Stowe Mountain Club pro shop (or a member) for a photo of the card, or find one on
   a golf-trip blog or review site.
2. Upload it to the release `data-spruce_peak` (GitHub > Releases > data-spruce_peak > Edit >
   drop the file > Update release), or attach it in the chat.

## 2. Back tees on 4, 8 and 13 (optional)

No tee box is mapped (OSM) or visible (2023 lidar pads, VCGI photo) at the Black card distance
on 4 (381 yd), 8 (606) and 13 (252). If you know where they are, either:

- tell me in words ("8's back tee is behind the 7th green, up the hill"), or
- send Google or Bing screenshots of each spot with at least 3 control points (pixel x/y and
  latitude/longitude of three recognisable points, e.g. bunker tips or path junctions, read off
  the map), per CLAUDE.md 5.1.
