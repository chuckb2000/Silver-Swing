The developer's calls of 2026-10-24 are in docs/courses/astoria.md ("The developer's calls"). The items below are the course chat's calls and the data's gaps, each with a recommendation.

1. **Routing is the course chat's** (OSM maps every green, tee box and fairway but no hole lines or pins). Greens H E M J S L C D P W I U K T N F G Q are holes 1-18 (proposed/osm_labelled.jpg, data/astoria/make_holes.py): each hole's tee boxes stand beside the previous green, every tee-to-green distance fits the Silver card (all 18 within 3.4 % from the box centres), the pars follow, and the course's descriptions agree (holes 2, 4, 8 and 17 the only east-west holes; 9 beside US 101 over the creek; 10 the par 3 by the clubhouse; 15 the smallest green). Lines run from the tee box via the fairway centreline (one bend on a dogleg, in the 250 yd landing zone) to the green centre. Recommendation: keep; the developer can correct any hole in the tracing desk.
