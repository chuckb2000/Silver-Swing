Correct the traced sand
  1. Tap a shape to select it. Drag its points to move them; Delete point removes the one you tapped.
  2. Remove shape if it isn't open sand (tap it again later to restore).
  3. Draw sand, then tap around a missing patch; tap the first point to close it.
  4. Save when done. Drag empty ground to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
