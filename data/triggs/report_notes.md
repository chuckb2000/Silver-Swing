## Other open items (Triggs Memorial)

Built by a course chat on 2026-10-20 with the standing defaults (docs/COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1); the developer went to bed mid-build and asked for questions to wait for the morning. The developer accepted every recommendation below on 2026-10-20 (22:29) and approved the logo.

1. **Scorecard ratings differ between the club's own sources.** Per-hole yardages, pars and stroke index come from the club's printed card (triggs.us images, release `data-triggs`); every row adds up to its printed total and OSM's hole tags match the Blue column on every hole. The card prints Blue 72.3/132, White 71.1/128, Gold 66.1/124, Red 71.3/124; the website's course page gives Blue 71.5/129, White 70.5/126, Red 72/124. Built with the printed card's 72.3/132. Recommendation: keep the card.
2. **Hole 15 Blue tee.** The OSM line starts on a forward box (457 yd). The box 34 m behind it, just past the 14th green (w677173986), measures 506 yd from its centre against the card's 508, but lies outside s06's 25 m search, so `edits.json` tags it `hole=15, tee_set=Blue`. This was the course chat's call (s06's line above says "the developer" for any tagged box). Built 500 yd (-1.6 %). Recommendation: accept.
3. **Three trees removed in front of the 15th tee** (s06b: 19 m, 14 m and 22 m tall, 23-44 m out), because a perfect drive at the middle of the fairway from that back box would hit them. This is the standing default (listed, not asked). Recommendation: accept; if the real 15th is a chute through those trees, say so and they go back via `keep_tee_trees: ["15"]`.
4. **Tee moves within their boxes** on 7 (179 -> 186 yd, card 191) and 12 (183 -> 195, card 200), logged in `adjustments.json`. Every hole is within 3 % of the card (6,529 vs 6,588, -0.9 %).
5. **Pins** at the centre of each green (`propose_pins.py`); OSM maps none. The game adds up to three more per green.
6. **Every penalty area is red** (standing default): five ponds and pools (`golf=water_hazard`, retagged `penalty=red`: beside 17, across 13, across 16, and two small pools on 7) and four `lateral_water_hazard` strips along the ditch crossing 11 and 12. The Woonasquatucket River, Manton Pond and the other water outside the boundary are out of bounds.
7. **Nine lidar trees dropped on playing surfaces** (spec 5.4), four of them along the east edge of the pond across 16, where the pond polygon reaches into the boundary tree line. Recommendation: accept.
8. **Greens:** the fitted tilt is clamped to 2.5 % on 12 greens; the max slope over a whole green reaches 4-6 % at the edges where the green blends into its surround (as at Wannamoisett and Franklin), and every pin area is <= 1.5 %.
9. **Practice green** by the clubhouse (w698136783) is a wrong green (relief required). The **driving range** inside the boundary (w1551632017) is out of bounds and drawn as a striped range (no `range_art`).
10. **The Button Hole short course** (way 185319906, south of Triggs, outside the world) and its features are dropped in `edits.json`.
11. **Trees** from the 2022 USGS RI statewide lidar (NOAA Data Access Viewer job, flown 30 March 2022, leaf-off), class 1: 8,690 trees, 2,398 on the course; conifers from the leaf-off last-return share: 9 % on the course, 20 % overall (as at Wannamoisett, same survey). OSM maps the neighbourhood's buildings well here (4,026), so only 84 crowns were dropped as unmapped roofs (`roof_last_share` 0.85).
12. **Tee sets not built:** White (6,327), Gold (5,499) and Red (5,374) are in `scorecard.csv`; only Blue is built (standing default).
13. **Architect and year:** the card says "Donald Ross 1933"; the City of Providence gives 1932 as the opening year. `course.json` gives both.
14. **Course card:** hole 12, its green beside the pond that 13 crosses (behind and right of the green as you play), with early-fall maples behind it; course chat's pick (`assets-src/cards/triggs.json`).
15. **Logo:** a Rhode Island Red rooster (developer's subject, 2026-10-20; `assets-src/course_logos/triggs.py`).
