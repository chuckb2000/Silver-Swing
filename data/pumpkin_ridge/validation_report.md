## Other open items (Pumpkin Ridge Golf Club)

Built 2026-10-24, both courses in one world (COURSE_BUILD.md 3.5). Standing defaults (COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1): Blue tees, all water red, out of bounds = the OSM course polygon (way 901988733, one polygon for both courses) with the driving range out of bounds and the club drives and paths inside it played as cart paths, trees from DOGAMI lidar, wind 1-7 mph. The course has its own theme, `pumpkin_ridge` (the developer allowed it in the build request). The items below are the course chat's calls, each with a recommendation.

1. **Ghost Creek 3 is a flag: 163 yd against 158 (+3.2 %).** The OSM boxes on 3 measure 204-225, 176-187, 160-169 and 146-156 yd (and shorter); none covers 158. s06 moved the tee to the front of the 160-169 box. Recommendation: accept the flag.
2. **Wetlands on Ghost Creek 3 and 4 are not penalty areas.** golfclubatlas.com and golfcoursegurus.com describe protected wetlands on 3 and carried from the 4th tee; OSM maps none, and the 2024 photo shows unmown native grass there with small lidar channels. Built as rough, drawn as native grass. A proposal to make the three native-grass areas the tee shots carry (61 m on 3; 28 m and 59 m on 4; 1.8 ha) red penalty areas is in `proposed/wetlands_gc34.geojson` (`make_wetland_proposal.py`), not in `edits.json`. Recommendation: keep them as rough unless you know them to be staked red; adding them is one line in `edits.json` and a rebuild.
3. **The marsh before the Witch Hollow 18 tee** (wiscogolfaddict 2015) is native grass on the photo and is not mapped; the tee shot clips 3.5 m of it 15 m out. Built as rough. Recommendation: keep.
4. **Native grass is art only.** About a third of the rough (34.6 ha in 390 areas) is drawn straw-gold where the 2024 photo shows it unmown (`make_fescue.py`, config `fescue_areas`, as at Gearhart); it plays as rough. Recommendation: keep.
5. **Trees the 2024 photo no longer shows:** 423 lidar crowns are dropped (`trees_need_ortho`), about 180 inside the boundary: clumps in the native areas by Ghost Creek 11/12 and 16/17, a row along the path west of the range, a few by Witch Hollow 17; outside, a plantation north of the course that has been cleared. Each cluster was checked on the photo (open ground or saplings now). Recommendation: keep.
6. **One edge tree on Witch Hollow 6.** A drive aimed straight down the OSM hole line at driver carry cuts the corner of the dogleg and clips a 36 m fir at the left edge of the fairway (local 1240, 1213; 165 m out). A perfect drive at the middle of the fairway (the default aim and the standing-default test) clears every tree on every hole of both courses, and s06b removed none. Recommendation: keep it.
7. **OSM tags that differ from the cards:** Ghost Creek 6 and 10 carry par 5 and par 4 (the card: 4 and 5; the line lengths, 346 and 491 yd, agree with the card); Witch Hollow's OSM `handicap` tags are a third stroke index (neither GolfPass's nor the older card's). s06 uses the cards. Recommendation: none needed.
8. **Scorecards are third-party (GolfPass).** Witch Hollow agrees with GolfPass's membership page and a 2015 blog; Ghost Creek's Blue+ row agrees hole for hole with hole19golf. An older Ghost Creek card (greenskeeper.org: Blue 6,451, another stroke index) is not used. Witch Hollow is the members' par 72 (the Korn Ferry Tour plays 71, the 14th as a par 4). The club's own cards were not read. Recommendation: accept; a photo of the club's printed cards would settle it.
9. **Tree types are inferred from crown shape** (no leaf-off data): 2,900 of 6,878 trees are drawn as Douglas firs (42 %). The broadleaves are drawn in autumn colour by sprite variant (oak olive-russet, bigleaf maple gold, vine maple orange): a drawing choice, not a survey of species.
10. **Ghost Creek 17's green has no random bump** (`green_tilt`, hole key 2017): with it the game found only two hole locations at <= 3 % round the cup. The 2.5 % tilt from the lidar is kept. Recommendation: none needed.
11. **Practice areas:** six practice greens by the two clubhouses play as wrong greens (relief required); the range (OSM `golf=practice`, retagged in `edits.json`) is out of bounds and drawn with stripes.
12. **Course card holes:** Witch Hollow 12 (rotating with 5, 14, 6) and Ghost Creek 18 (with 9, 11, 6), the scouting's picks; the title screen shows one view per place (Ghost Creek 18).
13. **Daily rotation:** `game/tests/unit/player.test.ts` requires every listed course to be in `DAILY_ROSTER`; it fails on this branch until the design chat adds the two layouts after the merge.
