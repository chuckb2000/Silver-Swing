## Other open items (Bandon Dunes)

The developer's calls (2026-10-20): Green tees; every water hazard red; out of bounds = the resort's course polygon (Pacific Dunes holes in it are in play, their greens wrong greens); trees from lidar; no separate no-play zones; wind 8-16 mph (rarely 3-8); a theme of its own with the yellow-flowering gorse; a seal logo. The developer was away for the build ("work without asking"), so the items below are the course chat's calls, each with a recommendation.

1. **Hole 1 is 394 yd against the card's 352 Green (+11.8 %), over the 8 % limit.** The only tee box at the start of the OSM hole line is a raised pad (lidar, 291 m2, plainly mown on the 2024 photo) 406 yd from the green: a Black-length tee (card 386, +5 %). No forward box is mapped or visible in the photo or the lidar. Options: (a) accept 394 yd (recommended: real geometry, one long par 4); (b) scale the hole by -5 % (the last-resort rule) to about 374 yd (+6 %); (c) the developer places the Green tee on the tracing desk.
2. **Tee boxes chosen by the course chat** (tagged in `edits.json`, each checked on the photo): hole 1 the lidar pad above; hole 2 a mapped box 33 m right of the line (153 yd, -1.1 %); hole 3 a mapped box 26 m left of the line (488 yd, -0.3 %); hole 11 a round mown pad left of the 10th green (lidar, 344 yd, -2.1 %; the OSM line starts 100 m farther south at boxes measuring 445-463 yd, longer than any card set); hole 12 the long mapped tee strip on the line. Two more pads of the 11th tee complex are drawn as tee boxes, not played.
3. **Flags fixed by moving the tee in its box** (5, 8, 12, 13): every hole but 1 is within 3 % after the move.
4. **Gorse** (developer: iconic): 14,549 bushes over 16.9 ha of dense scrub classified in the 2024 orthophoto (11.7 ha inside the boundary), drawn as yellow-flowering gorse from the theme's own bush sheet (trees.json c: 3; `pipeline/gorse.py`, `assets-src/trees_bandon_dunes.py`). Inside the boundary the ground under the thickets is the gorse surface (17): a penal lie, at most 20-50 yd out (design chat, `design/gorse`). The photo shows dense dark scrub, not which shrub it is: species and bloom are flair. Recommendation: keep; look at it in play.
5. **Trees:** 3,692 lidar trees (1,182 on the course), all drawn as shore pine (the crown-shape rule called 86 % of them deciduous; the broad, wind-sheared shore pines are the course's trees and the broadleaf scrub is the gorse). 407 lidar trees whose crowns are not canopy in the 2024 photo were dropped (`trees_need_ortho`): the DOGAMI lidar predates the Bandon Preserve and parts of the Punchbowl. No tee shot needed a tree removed.
6. **The beach** below the bluff is drawn as sand from the lidar (`proposed/beach.geojson`, ground below 8 m between the sea and the bluff; OSM's beach polygon starts south-west of the world). Scenery only: it is out of bounds either way.
7. **Water:** the one pond inside the boundary (w777788176, near the 11th) is a red penalty area. Chrome Lake and Round Lake lie outside the boundary and are out of bounds.
8. **No-play zones** (the card's local rule on 4, 5, 6, 12, 13, 16, 17 and 18): not modelled (developer). Most of the bluff they protect is outside the boundary, so it is out of bounds already.
9. **Wind 8-16 mph** (developer). The game draws each round's wind from one range; the rare calm round (3-8 mph) needs a design-chat change (docs/courses/bandon_dunes.md).
