## Other open items (RedTail Golf Center)

Built 2026-10-22. Standing defaults (COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1): Blue tees, all water red, out of bounds = the OSM course boundary (way 156976873) with the driving range out of bounds and the parking aisles and paths inside it played as cart paths, trees from DOGAMI lidar, `pnw` theme, wind 1-7 mph. Rating/slope from the club card, 72.6/130 (developer, 2026-10-22). The items below are the course chat's calls, each with a recommendation.

1. **Hole 3 is a flag: 306 yd against 322 (-4.9 %).** OSM maps three boxes on hole 3, measuring about 345, 304 and 281 yd; the card has five sets (Black 374, Blue 322, White 304, Red 280). s06 took the White-length box (w871730441) and moved the tee to its back (306 yd); the back box (w871730440) would be +7 %. The 2024 photo shows no box between them. (The line "accepted for play" in the table's open items is s06's standard wording; it needs the developer's call.) Recommendation: accept the flag.
2. **Stroke index:** the card's men's row (Black/Blue/White) is used. OSM tags hole 1 with handicap 10, the same as hole 18; the card says 13. s06's open-item line about it names "GolfPass" and "spec 11.4", text written for Oswego Lake: the source here is the club card. Request to the design chat: make that line name the course's own scorecard source.
3. **Two edge trees over the hole lines of 7 and 10.** OSM draws both lines along the left edge of the fairway; a perfect drive straight down the line clips a 21 m broadleaf on 7 (local 815, 838; 188 m out) and a 26 m broadleaf on 10 (451, 465; 182 m out). Both stand in the left rough on the 2024 photo. A perfect drive at the middle of the fairway (the default aim, and the standing default's test) clears every tree on every hole, so neither was removed. Recommendation: keep both; remove either with `drop_trees` if they get in the way in play.
4. **Tree types are inferred from crown shape** (DOGAMI canopy height model, no leaf-off data): 1,298 of 5,584 trees are drawn as conifers (23 %). The 2024 photo shows rows of tall Douglas firs along holes 4, 5 and 10 that the shape rule partly draws as broadleaves. Recommendation: accept for now; typing crowns from the orthophoto would be a new config key in s05 (and a `make regress` run).
5. **Cart paths:** two cart-path loops are mapped as closed ways (w1206745336, 2.5 km round holes 2-7, and w870641352 by the clubhouse). The Overpass export made them polygons, which s01 read as cart-path areas (8.5 ha over six holes). `make_edits.py` writes them back as lines (`proposed/cartpath_loops.geojson`, `edits.json`); a unit test checks that the middle of every hole is not cart path.
6. **Water:** all seven OSM `golf=water_hazard` ponds are tagged `penalty=red` in `edits.json` (s10 reads `water_hazard` as yellow otherwise). OSM also maps the pond by 16 and 17 a second time as `natural=water` (r7368287); the hazard covers it. No stream crosses the course (the channel map of the DEM shows none); the intermittent stream south-east of 17 runs in a culvert outside the boundary.
7. **Trees over the ponds:** 60 lidar crowns whose tops stand over a pond polygon were dropped (spec 5.4); they are bank trees leaning over the water. One more stood on a tee of hole 1 (w825466726).
8. **Practice areas:** four practice greens by the clubhouse and range play as wrong greens (relief required). The driving range is out of bounds and drawn with stripes, as at most courses (Oregon Golf Club draws its range as rough with `range_art`). Recommendation: keep.
9. **Scorecard:** the club card printed 6/2012 (Golf ScoreCards Inc.). golfingrecord.com lists the same Blue yardages. A current card would confirm nothing has changed since 2012. Recommendation: none needed.
10. **Lidar date:** the DOGAMI mosaic services give no project year for the export. Recommendation: none needed for play.
11. **Daily rotation:** `game/tests/unit/player.test.ts` requires every course on the list to be in `DAILY_ROSTER` (`game/src/data/dailyRoster.ts`), so it fails on this branch until the design chat adds `redtail` there (COURSE_BUILD.md 0: the design chat adds each merged course to the rotation).
