## Other open items (Gearhart Golf Links)

The developer's calls of 2026-10-20 are in docs/courses/gearhart.md ("The developer's calls"). The items below are the course chat's calls and the data's gaps, each with a recommendation.

1. **Holes 14 and 15 renumbered** (developer): OSM numbers holes 14, 15, 16 as 15, 16, 16; `edits.json` sets ref 14 and 15 on ways 1322722942 and 1322722943. Pars, lengths and the order of play then match the card.
2. **Gearhart Lane and the three parking lots are out of bounds** (developer, the card's local rule). The lane through `ob_highways: ["tertiary"]`; the lots are cut out of the boundary (`make_boundary.py`, 6,666 m2). The short clubhouse driveway between the two clubhouse lots (w1322656805, 39 m) stays a cart path with free relief. Recommendation: keep.
3. **Lidar trees gone since the 2013-15 renovation are removed** (developer): the DOGAMI canopy model predates the tree removal, so 737 of the 1,067 lidar trees inside the boundary stand where the OSIP 2024 photo shows fescue (`check_trees.py`, `drop_trees`; their canopy is cleared too). 82 uncertain trees (score 0.15-0.40) are kept. Check picture: `proposed/trees_photo_check.png`. Recommendation: keep; the bare-earth DEM is the same pre-renovation flight, so reshaped ground (new bunkers, rebuilt greens) may differ slightly from the 2009 heights.
4. **Hole 4 and hole 15 Black tees on their long teeing grounds** (course chat): hole 4's box w1322656824 (46 x 6 m, 130-180 yd) and hole 15's box w1322653215 (980 m2, 72-126 yd) hold the Black yardages, but their centroids are over 8 % short, so `edits.json` tags them `hole`, `tee_set=Black` and s06 moves the tee along each box (176.7 and 113.0 yd). s06's report line says "assigned by the developer"; it was the course chat's call. The lidar pad at 173 yd on hole 4 is rough beside the cart path in the 2024 photo, not used. Recommendation: keep.
5. **Fescue areas from the 2024 photo** (course chat, art only): 83 areas, 15.6 ha (61 % of the course's rough), by the colour rule in `make_fescue.py`. They change only how the rough is drawn (`fescue_areas`); all rough plays the same. OSM's `golf=rough` patches cover the front nine's fescue but little of the back nine's, so the photo is used instead. Recommendation: keep; the developer can redraw any area that looks wrong.
6. **Tree types are flair:** s05 splits crowns by shape (1,014 narrow "conifers", 6,119 broad). The `gearhart` theme draws narrow crowns as Sitka spruce and broad ones as wind-swept shore pine, every third broad crown (index mod 3 = 2) as red alder. No leaf-off data; species are not surveyed.
7. **Ratings are GolfPass's** (unverified; the card prints none): Black 70.8/137, The Stones 72.7/139, White 69.3/130, Green 66.1/121. GolfPass also lists a Craic set (5,896) not on the 2020 card, and its White row differs from the card on hole 7 (273 vs 328). Recommendation: keep the card's yardages and GolfPass's ratings.
8. **Greens 8 and 18 are steeper than the clamp:** fitted tilts 3.7 % and 4.2 % are clamped to 2.5 % (max slope 5.7 % and 5.1 % with the undulations); the area within 3 m of every centre pin stays at or under 1.5 %.
