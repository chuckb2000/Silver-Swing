Decisions the developer made on 2026-10-19 are in DECISIONS.md ("Wannamoisett"). The developer accepted every recommendation below on 2026-10-19:

Ratings: 72.8/135 for the 6,732 set (screenshot and GolfPass tee table), 71.3/132 for the Blue set (GolfPass tee table, listed there as "White 6,427"); the GolfPass card prints Black 73.5/130 and Blue 71.9/127. Accepted; if the developer gets the current printed card, update `scorecard.csv` and the ratings and rerun from s06.
2. **Black 12 plays 195 yd vs 211 (-7.4 %, flag).** There is no Black box for 12 in OSM or the lidar (the two raised pads `propose_tees.py --wide=12` found are a bunker lip and a mound), so Black plays from the back of the mapped box, as far back as it goes. Every other hole of both sets is within 3 % after moving the virtual tee within its box (9 moves, `adjustments.json`). Accepted.
3. **Pins** are at the centre of each green (`propose_pins.py`); OSM has no pins.
4. **Penalty areas are both red** (developer): the pond beside 14 and the pond crossed by holes 1 and 9. OSM cuts the hole-14 pond out of the course relation as an inner ring, which would make it out of bounds; config `join_boundary` fills it, so it is a penalty area inside the boundary.
5. **Clubhouse** is mapped `golf=clubhouse` + `building=yes`; s01 reads the golf key first, so the OSM outline is added as a building (`propose_buildings.py`, `edits.json`): it is drawn and gives obstruction relief.
6. **Trees come from the 2022 USGS RI statewide lidar,** flown leaf-off 26-30 March 2022. The project has no vegetation class, so trees are taken from class 1 (unclassified), which also holds buildings. OSM maps only ~50 buildings here, not the neighbourhood houses, so their roofs read as crowns: 1,824 crowns whose returns were >= 85 % last returns (solid surfaces) are dropped as roofs (`roof_last_share`); inside the course they all sit on the clubhouse and mapped buildings. Type from the leaf-off last-return share: 15 % pines on the course, 26 % off it (yards with arborvitae, yew and white pine; some may be roof edges, which can't be told apart without imagery). Accepted; if a stand looks wrong in the game, name it and it can be dropped with `drop_trees`.
7. **Practice greens** (two, by the clubhouse) are wrong greens (relief required). No driving range is mapped.
8. **Course card** (COURSE_BUILD.md section 12): hole 12, the par-3 green beside its big flashed bunker with turning maples behind (confirmed by the developer). To change it, edit `assets-src/course_cards.json` and run `python3 assets-src/course_cards.py wannamoisett`.
