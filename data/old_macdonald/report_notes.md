## Other open items (Old Macdonald)

Built 2026-10-21 together with Pacific Dunes and Sheep Ranch (shared downloads, GitHub release pacific-dunes-data). Standing defaults (COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1): all water red, out of bounds = the resort polygon w362513477 (the Pacific Dunes and Bandon Dunes ground inside it is in play, their greens wrong greens), trees from lidar; wind 8-16 mph and the bandon_dunes theme with gorse as the developer set for Bandon Dunes. **Tees: Black** (developer, 2026-10-21): the OSM hole lines start on the tees ("the tees are at the end of the red line"), and measured from there the course matches the Black card (7,143 yd from the line starts against 6,944), not Green (6,320, the 4.1 default). The items below are the course chat's calls, each with a recommendation.

1. **Tee boxes.** OSM maps tee polygons only on holes 2-6 and 18. Where no mapped box at the line start measures within 8 % of the Black card, a 12 x 8 m box aligned with the line stands on the line start (`make_tees_linestart.py`; 15 holes). The box size is the course chat's. Recommendation: keep; correct any in the tracing desk if a tee looks wrong in play.
2. **Holes 1 and 13: the line starts are 16-17 % beyond the Black card** (395 and 405 yd against 341 and 346). Their boxes stand where the Black distance falls on the line, 49 and 54 m ahead of the line start (now 339 and 346 yd). Recommendation: confirm, or say where the tees are.
3. **Flags, measured from the line starts** (3-5 % after s06 moved each tee inside its box): hole 2 174/181 yd (-4.1 %), 11 466/445 (+4.7 %), 12 245/237 (+3.3 %), 16 473/455 (+4.0 %). Recommendation: accept (the line starts are the developer's tees).
4. **Double green 5/10.** OSM maps one green (w697052065, 3,126 m2) that both lines end on, with a pin for each hole. It is cut in two along the perpendicular bisector of the two pins (`split_double_greens.py`), so each hole has its own part, pin and putting views (as at Langdon Farms). The parts are shaped as two greens, which leaves a seam between them (a design-chat request). Recommendation: confirm that 5 and 10 share the green.
5. **Hole 8's pin** is at the visual centre of the 69 m Biarritz green; OSM's pin stood 16 m from it, outside the close-up putting view. The game adds three more pins round the green.
6. **The lidar was flown in 2008, before Old Macdonald was built** (2009-2010). The ground is the pre-construction dunes: greens are shaped by script and tees levelled anyway, bunkers come from OSM. The lidar is DOGAMI's OLC South Coast project (DOGAMI labels it 2009; the USGS data reports give the flights as June-October 2008). It is the only survey covering the three courses: DOGAMI's 2020 South Coast project has no data here, and the USGS OR_Western_D23 point cloud starts at about 43.223 N (checked 2026-10-21). 248 lidar trees whose crowns are not canopy in the 2024 photo were dropped (`trees_need_ortho`).
7. **Gorse:** 32,138 bushes over 37.7 ha of scrub classified in the 2024 orthophoto (20.1 ha inside the boundary), on the gorse surface (17). Recommendation: keep; look at it in play.
8. **Water:** Elbow Lake (w694622373, golf=water_hazard in OSM, yellow by default) is red (standing default).
9. **The north photo tile** is used from the copy sent in the chat (PNG); its original JPEG is not in the release yet (release.json).
