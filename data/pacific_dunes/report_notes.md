## Other open items (Pacific Dunes)

Built 2026-10-21 together with Old Macdonald and Sheep Ranch (shared downloads, GitHub release pacific-dunes-data). Standing defaults (COURSE_BUILD.md 4.1): Green tees, all water red, out of bounds = the resort polygon w362513477 (the Bandon Dunes and Old Macdonald ground inside it is in play, their greens wrong greens), trees from lidar; wind 8-16 mph and the bandon_dunes theme with gorse as the developer set for Bandon Dunes. The developer kept Green for this course (2026-10-21). The items below are the course chat's calls, each with a recommendation.

1. **Tee boxes picked by the course chat on holes 4, 8, 12, 15, 16 and 17.** The OSM lines of these holes start at forward boxes (13-30 % short of the Green card). Each hole has a mapped box behind its line start, 54-76 m off the line, that measures within 2.3 % of the Green card (459/449, 360/369, 507/507, 508/504, 334/338, 191/189 yd); each was checked on the 2024 photo (a mown tee behind the line start, facing the green). Recommendation: keep.
2. **Flags fixed by moving the tee inside its box** (5, 11, 13, 14): every hole is within 3 % (course 6,130 yd against 6,142).
3. **Gorse:** 13,677 bushes over 15.9 ha of dense scrub classified in the 2024 orthophoto (14.8 ha inside the boundary), on the gorse surface (17) as at Bandon Dunes. The photo shows dense dark scrub, not which shrub it is. Recommendation: keep; look at it in play.
4. **Trees:** 2,626 lidar trees (1,862 inside the boundary), all drawn as shore pine; 129 lidar trees whose crowns are not canopy in the 2024 photo were dropped (`trees_need_ortho`). The lidar is DOGAMI's OLC South Coast project (DOGAMI labels it 2009; the USGS data reports give the flights as June-October 2008). It is the only survey covering the three courses: DOGAMI's 2020 South Coast project has no data here, and the USGS OR_Western_D23 point cloud starts at about 43.223 N (checked 2026-10-21). No tree was removed for a tee shot.
5. **No-play zones** (the card's local rule on 4, 11 and 13) are not modelled, as at Bandon Dunes.
6. **Water:** no penalty area lies in this world; Elbow Lake (w694622373, by Old Macdonald) is exported red.
7. **The north photo tile** is used from the copy sent in the chat (PNG); its original JPEG is not in the release yet (release.json). If the JPEG decodes to different pixels, the gorse and tree checks are rerun.
